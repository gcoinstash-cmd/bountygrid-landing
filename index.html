Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

How does BountyGrid OS prevent broken or low-quality code?

Every issue is executed in an isolated sandbox environment. Our workflow requires writing a failing test reproducing the issue first and verifying that 100% of preexisting tests pass before a senior engineer approves the Pull Request.

How do monthly retainers work?

Retainers are billed monthly via Stripe. You grant repository access, and our engineering team continuously monitors your issue queue, triages backlog tickets, updates dependencies, and opens clean PRs for your team to merge.

Can you handle custom full-stack features?

Yes. Our Product Sprint package is designed for discrete, multi-file feature additions, database migrations, or third-party API integrations delivered in 1–2 weeks.
